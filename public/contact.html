Response Times

We aim to respond to all inquiries within:

  • General Support: Within 24 hours (business days)
  • Urgent Issues: Within 4 hours (during business hours, IST)
  • Privacy/Data Requests: Within 48 hours
  • Billing Issues: Within 24 hours

Business Hours

Our support team is available Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST (Indian Standard Time). We are closed on Indian national holidays and weekends, though urgent issues are monitored.

Report a Security Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability in Advocate DigiDiary, please report it responsibly by emailing security@advocate-digidiary.law. Do not disclose security issues publicly. We will acknowledge your report within 24 hours and work with you to resolve the issue.
