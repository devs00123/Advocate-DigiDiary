Refund Policy

Effective Date: September 20, 2026 • Last Updated: September 20, 2026

At Advocate DigiDiary, we strive to provide a reliable and valuable Legal Practice Management platform. This Refund Policy outlines the terms under which refunds may be issued for subscriptions and payments made to Advocate DigiDiary.

1. Free Tier

Advocate DigiDiary offers a free tier with core features. No payment is required to access the free tier, and therefore no refund applies.

2. Paid Subscriptions

If you have subscribed to a paid plan:

  • 14-Day Trial: New subscribers are offered a 14-day free trial. You may cancel before the trial ends without incurring any charge.
  • Monthly Subscriptions: Refund requests for monthly subscriptions must be made within 7 days of the billing date. Refunds are granted at our sole discretion.
  • Annual Subscriptions: Refund requests for annual subscriptions must be made within 30 days of the billing date. A pro-rated refund may be issued for the unused portion.

3. Non-Refundable Items

  • Subscriptions that have been active for more than the refund window (7 days for monthly, 30 days for annual).
  • Add-on services or premium features already consumed.
  • Payments made for third-party services integrated through the Platform.
  • Any fees paid to courts, tribunals, or government authorities through the Platform's expense tracking.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact our support team at support@advocate-digidiary.law with the following information:

  • Your registered email address
  • Date of payment
  • Amount paid
  • Reason for refund request

We will review your request within 5 business days and respond with a resolution.

5. Processing Refunds

Approved refunds will be processed within 10-15 business days to the original payment method. You will receive an email confirmation once the refund has been initiated.

6. Cancellation vs. Refund

Cancelling your subscription stops future billing but does not automatically generate a refund for the current billing period. To receive a refund for the current period, you must submit a separate refund request within the applicable refund window.

7. Contact Us

For refund requests or questions about this policy:

Email: support@advocate-digidiary.law
Platform: Settings → Billing & Subscription
